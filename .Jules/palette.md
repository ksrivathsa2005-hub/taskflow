## 2026-03-20 - LoginComponent Accessibility Improvements
**Learning:** Explicit association between labels and inputs via `id` and `for` attributes is crucial for screen reader users to understand the context of each form field. Relying solely on visual proximity or implicit nesting (which was not used here) is insufficient for accessibility. Additionally, interactive selection controls like role buttons should use `aria-pressed` to communicate their active state, and status messages (errors/success) should use `role="alert"` and `aria-live` to ensure they are announced immediately or politely by assistive technologies.
**Action:** Always ensure all form inputs have unique `id`s and corresponding `<label for="...">` elements. Use `aria-pressed` for toggle-like buttons and `aria-live` for dynamic content like alerts.
