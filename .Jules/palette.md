## 2026-06-13 - [Accessible Star Rating Pattern]
**Learning:** For star rating systems, using `role="radiogroup"` on the container and `role="radio"` on individual buttons provides the best experience for screen reader users. Including `aria-label` for each star (e.g., "4 stars") and `aria-checked` ensures clarity of selection.
**Action:** Use this pattern for all rating-based inputs to ensure consistency and accessibility.
